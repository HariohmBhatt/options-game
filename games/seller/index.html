Seller's Week

How it works
  • You sell an option: the buyer pays you the premium now, and your broker blocks margin.
  • Quiet days earn you time value; a big move the wrong way can cost many times the premium.
  • Rounds 1–3 are naked (no hedge); rounds 4–5 add a bought option that caps your loss.

Educational only. Prices are simulated. Not financial advice.
