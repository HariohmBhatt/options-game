5. Seller's Week

How it works. This time you sell the option: the buyer pays you the premium up front. Your broker blocks margin (about ₹1.25 lakh per NIFTY lot) from your cash as a safety deposit while the trade is open. If the option expires worthless on Tuesday at 15:30 you keep the whole premium, and every quiet day earns you some of it. If NIFTY moves hard the wrong way you pay the buyer the difference, and for a sold CE there is no limit to how much that can be (a sold PE can lose up to the strike × 65, if NIFTY fell to 0). That is why beginners are told not to sell naked options: buying a further-away option as a hedge (a credit spread) caps the loss.
