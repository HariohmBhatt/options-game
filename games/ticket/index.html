1. The Ticket

How it works. An option is a ticket that gives you the right, but not the obligation, to trade a stock at a fixed price (the strike) until a deadline (the expiry, here 10 days). A CALL is the right to buy at the strike, so it pays when the price goes up; a PUT is the right to sell at the strike, so it pays when the price goes down. You pay a small fee up front for the ticket (the premium). If the trade never makes sense you just don't use it, so the most you can lose is that premium.
