1. NIFTY Weekly

How it works. An option is the right, but not the obligation, to trade the NIFTY 50 index at a fixed level (the strike) until expiry, which for NIFTY is every Tuesday at 15:30. A CE (call) gains when NIFTY rises above the strike; a PE (put) gains when NIFTY falls below it. You pay the premium up front for one lot of 65 units, and you can sell the option back any day before expiry, or hold it: at expiry it is worth only how far it is in the money, and worth zero otherwise. Part of every premium is time value, which melts each day, and each trade also pays brokerage, STT (a tax on sales) and small exchange fees. The most you can ever lose is the premium plus those charges.

Educational only. Prices are simulated. Not financial advice.
