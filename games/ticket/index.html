1. NIFTY Weekly

How it works. An option is the right, but not the obligation, to trade the NIFTY 50 index at a fixed level (the strike) at expiry, which for NIFTY is every Tuesday at 15:30; it is settled in cash, not in shares. A CE (call) gains when NIFTY rises above the strike; a PE (put) gains when NIFTY falls below it. You pay the premium up front for 1–3 lots of 65 units each, buying at the ask (a little above the fair mid price) and selling back at the bid (a little below it), any day before expiry. If you hold to expiry the option is worth only how far it is in the money, and zero otherwise. Part of every premium is time value, which melts each day, and it also rises or falls with India VIX, which moves day to day. Each trade pays brokerage, STT (a tax on sales) and small fees, and the most you can ever lose is the premium plus those charges.

Educational only. Prices are simulated. Not financial advice.
