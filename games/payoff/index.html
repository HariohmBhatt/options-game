Payoff Painter

How to read this chart

Left to right is the stock price on expiry day; up and down is your profit or loss. Above the middle line you made money, below it you lost money. The flat part is what happens when the option expires worthless: a buyer loses the premium they paid, a seller keeps the premium they collected. The line bends at the strike, and where it crosses zero is the breakeven.
