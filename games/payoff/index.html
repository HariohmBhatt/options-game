How to read this chart

NIFTY 50 is at 24,000 and this week's options expire on Tuesday. Left to right is where NIFTY closes on expiry day; up and down is your profit or loss in rupees on 1 lot (65 units). Above the middle line you made money, below it you lost money.

A CE (call) is the right to buy NIFTY at the strike; a PE (put) is the right to sell at the strike. The flat part is what happens when the option expires worthless: a buyer loses the premium they paid, a seller keeps the premium they collected. The line bends at the strike, and where it crosses zero is the breakeven. Each point NIFTY moves past that is ₹65 on 1 lot.
