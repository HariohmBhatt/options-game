How to read this chart

NIFTY 50 is at 24,000 and this week's options expire on Tuesday. Left to right is where NIFTY closes on expiry day; up and down is your profit or loss in rupees on 1 lot (65 units). Above the middle line you made money, below it you lost money.

The strike is the NIFTY level written into the contract, and the premium is the option's price. NIFTY options are cash-settled: on 1 lot, a CE (call) pays ₹65 for every point NIFTY ends above the strike, and a PE (put) pays ₹65 for every point NIFTY ends below the strike. The flat part is what happens when the option expires worthless: a buyer loses the premium they paid, a seller keeps the premium they collected. The line bends at the strike, and where it crosses zero is the breakeven.

Every order also pays charges, including STT (Securities Transaction Tax, charged when you sell).
