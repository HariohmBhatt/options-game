How it works
  • Calls (CE) are on the left, puts (PE) on the right, strikes in the middle.
  • OI is contracts open, IV is the yearly move a price assumes, LTP is the premium per unit.
  • Shaded cells are in the money. One lot is 65 units, so it costs LTP × 65.

Educational only. Prices and OI are simulated. Not financial advice.
