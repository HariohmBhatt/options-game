4. Read the Chain

How it works. An option chain lists every strike of one expiry in a single table, the screen NSE traders watch all day. Calls (CE) are on the left, puts (PE) on the right, and the strike sits in the middle. OI (open interest) is how many contracts of 65 are open right now; chg OI is how much that changed today. IV (implied volatility) is how big a move the option's price assumes, in % per year. LTP (last traded price) is the premium per unit, so one lot costs LTP × 65. VIX (India VIX) is the volatility the market expects for NIFTY, in % per year, worked out from option prices. Shaded cells are in the money (ITM: a call whose strike is below NIFTY, a put whose strike is above it), and the outlined row is ATM, the strike nearest to NIFTY.
