3. Expiry Tuesday

How it works. NIFTY weekly options expire every Tuesday, and on that day you can buy options that die the same afternoon (0DTE, zero days to expiry). An option's price is intrinsic value (what it is worth if it expired right now) plus time value (what you pay for the chance NIFTY moves your way before the end). Your strike is the NIFTY level the option is about. Cheap out-of-the-money options (a CE strike above NIFTY, a PE strike below it) are almost all time value, and on expiry day that time value melts away by the minute (theta). At 15:30 every open option settles: in the money (NIFTY past your strike) it pays its intrinsic value, otherwise it expires worthless. One lot is 65 units, so a ₹10 premium costs ₹650 plus charges.

Educational only. Prices are simulated. Not financial advice.
