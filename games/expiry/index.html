3. Expiry Tuesday

How it works. NIFTY weekly options expire every Tuesday, so on that day you can buy options that die the same afternoon (0DTE, zero days to expiry). An option's price is intrinsic value (what it is worth if it expired right now) plus time value (what you pay for the chance NIFTY moves past your strike, the NIFTY level the option is about), and on expiry day time value melts by the minute (theta). You can watch first: +5 min and +30 min move the clock before you buy, and you get one trade per round. You buy at the ask and sell at the bid, a little lower, and one lot is 65 units, so a ₹10 premium costs ₹650 plus charges. Far out-of-the-money options (a CE strike well above NIFTY, a PE strike well below it) are cheap because they will probably expire worthless. At 15:30 every open option settles: in the money (NIFTY past your strike) it pays its intrinsic value, otherwise ₹0.
